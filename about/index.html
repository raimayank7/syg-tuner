About Tuner

Tuner by ShareYourGroup is a free instrument tuner that runs in your browser. It listens through the microphone, names the note being played and shows how far it is from being in tune. It has pages for guitar, bass, ukulele, violin, viola, cello, mandolin and banjo with reference tones for every string, alternate guitar tunings, a tone generator, a chart of note frequencies, a page on Sa, sargam and tanpura tuning, and guides to tuning.

Your microphone and your privacy

The microphone is opened only after you tap Start, and only to measure pitch. The sound is analysed in your browser and discarded at once. It is never recorded, stored or sent to us or to anyone else. Tapping Stop, closing the tab or switching away releases the microphone.

Where the content comes from

The tuner was written for this site. Its pitch detector uses a well-known published idea (a normalised difference function, as in the YIN method) in our own code, and was tested with 1,584 computer-made tones from 30 Hz to 1,300 Hz, all of which it placed within one cent. Note frequencies are calculated by the site's build program in twelve-tone equal temperament from A4 = 440 Hz, the international standard tuning pitch. The tunings listed (standard tunings of the eight instruments, common alternate guitar and banjo tunings, the usual tanpura tuning and the kali / safed key names) are the ones in general use among players; they were written from knowledge in November 2026 and are not taken from any one book or website. All guide text is our own.

Limits

A phone microphone in a noisy room is not a laboratory instrument. Expect accuracy of a few cents, less on very low notes. Voice ranges on the Sa page are rough guides only.
